Key Design Decisions
Menu ≠ Inventory
menu_item_variant links to inventory_item via menu_variant_ingredient. Hazy IPA (M) and (L) deduct different volumes from the same keg row — no double inventory entries.
Visit ≠ Customer
visit.customer_id is nullable. A session opens on QR scan; the customer is linked post-hoc if they consent. Order belongs to visit, never directly to a customer.
IAM — Most Permissive Wins
Permissions are the union of all active group + individual policies. user_group_membership.valid_until handles time-bound elevation (e.g. bartender → manager for one shift).
Payment Idempotency
payment.gateway_tx_id carries a UNIQUE constraint. GB Prime Pay may retry webhooks — the service checks this before settling to prevent double-payment.
Snapshots on Order Items
order_item.unit_price_snapshot and order_item_modifier.price_delta_snapshot freeze prices at order time. Menu price changes never retroactively alter a bill.
Audit Log — Append Only
No UPDATE or DELETE ever touches audit_log. Voids, comps, and price changes leave a before/after jsonb payload trail. Pair with a DB trigger or TypeORM subscriber.

Audit Log
Append-only. No UPDATE or DELETE ever.
audit_log append-only
PK iduuid
entityvarchar(100)
entity_iduuid
actionvarchar(20)
changed_fieldsjsonb?
changed_byuuid
changed_attimestamptz
Diff-only pattern — changedFields stores {field: {from, to}} for scalar changes only; null on CREATE. Populated automatically by TypeORM AuditSubscriber via @nestjs/cls userId. No actor_type — always a staff user. No full snapshots — query by field name to see who changed status, price, etc.
